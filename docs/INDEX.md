`PROTOCOL.md` is the only current canonical plan. Reports and literature files explain or support it;
they do not independently authorize execution or override its rules.

Protocol 1.7 asks, for one exact model and one R0a-frozen 24-template common-content prompt frame,
whether registered painter-name requests meet availability/content-adherence requirements and,
among technically analyzable near-copy-excluded outputs, whether the generated distribution
reproduces the `q*`-content-standardized distribution in the painter's R0a-randomized sealed-
confirmation population of eligible digital painting surrogates. The study uses four broad scene
groups; its former six narrow submotifs are nonbinding diagnostics. Every work in every frozen real
population is analyzed: 72 development, 108 qualification, and at least 180 confirmation works per
painter, for 288, 432, and at least 720 overall and at least 1,440 internal. Optional external
replication is a complete 96-work census per painter (384 overall), raising both acquired and
analyzed real-work counts to at least 1,824. A separate auxiliary same-work independent-capture
census has at least 32 physical works—at least eight per painter and two independent captures per
work—and is outside every total.

Before any active content label is read, R0a hashes every byte-exact artist-free and named-placeholder
string, punctuation, language, negative prompt, insertion point, painter-name substitution table,
scene label, content value, and render contract for all 12 complete 24-template frames; G0 may verify
but never rewrite them. After painter-level population assignment, R0a selects one using the scene-
group proportions plus exactly
five predeclared binary visible-property means. Every named condition and shared painter-free control
must receive the same `R` in every template, making the four-painter request count `120R`.
The former `R=16`/1,920-request scenario is retired: even the impossible best case requires
`R>=25` and at least 3,000 requests when every repetition is its own auditable independent unit,
while the actual Bonferroni allocation, independence-unit audit, and simulation require a larger,
still-unfrozen `R`. A fixed deterministic local map uses independent template-specific IID uniform
seed lists with replacement. An opaque/remote endpoint instead uses `C` equal-size common-shock
units, each carrying `L` complete template×condition waves, with `R=C*L`. R0a freezes a
jointly feasible full-population entropy projection
`q*` for every complete intended real population; `q*` is primary, joint convex-hull, weight-cap,
and effective-sample-size gates apply, and it is never refit. The uniform complete population is a
mandatory sensitivity. One weighted-median/IQR transform is fit to the equal-painter mixture of all
four `q*`-weighted complete development populations and reused everywhere. A separately frozen,
binding realized-content entropy projection uses blind output labels, the same support gates, and
cannot rescue the primary result; neither construction claims exact joint-profile matching.
The 3,190 Wikidata items are discovery candidates. The separate 43-record official-source artifact
is traceable live-item evidence, not a reproducible as-of-date census; neither artifact contains an
admitted outdoor-place work. Active-study admissions, image downloads, registered generation
requests, generated outputs, and results remain zero; R0a is NO-GO. Internal GO requires 360
eligible candidates per painter and at least 24 in every broad scene group. External registration
requires another 96 unopened-source candidates per painter and at least eight per broad scene group,
or 456 combined per painter.

R0a coding uses masked, frozen eligibility derivatives with long side at most 512 pixels. This is
logged pixel exposure and remains firewalled from analysts; analysis-resolution qualification/final
files remain inaccessible and the final reference stays sealed through G1a. Before adjudication,
three-way eligibility agreement must be at least 0.90 per painter and each coder's ambiguous share at
most 0.10. On the union-eligible denominator, broad-scene and each five-property three-state contrast
must reach 0.85, repeated in every assigned internal and registered-external population; each coder's
season/illumination/depth indeterminate share must be at most 0.20 in every required scope. Failure is
R0a NO-GO and adjudication cannot erase it. In G1b, all sealed-confirmation and technically analyzable
generated images are double-coded; raw condition-specific receipts are sealed before third-coder
consensus, and failure makes the affected endpoint inconclusive. One painter-level
random permutation assigns the development, qualification, and confirmation exposure roles; it does
not create later probability subsamples. R0b releases each complete qualification census and cannot
add or redefine the population. Primary energy uses a `q*`-weighted real-census summation inside the
generated–real term, a real–real finite component, and an equal-template generated U-statistic: only
the two real-side components are exact; generator expectations remain estimated. Generator-
only resampling holds the real population and its
weights fixed: local deterministic inference resamples whole seed-condition vectors within template;
opaque/remote inference resamples only whole balanced common-shock units and preserves every
template count. Generated quantiles preserve equal template mass by weighting each output
`1/(24 m_t)`. Real-work IPW, SRSWOR inference, and real-work bootstraps are not active methods.
The continuous max statistic excludes rates: availability, adherence, and copy use a separately
alpha-allocated Bonferroni weighted-Hoeffding construction with conservative ratio bounds. A full
rate endpoint counts four one-sided events in `M_rate`: `A` lower, `A` upper, `J` lower, and `K`
upper. The same G0 independence partition governs rates and continuous inference, and the rate bound
uses `sum_c W_c^2`; remote request IDs and timestamps do not establish independence, so plausible
provider/batch/backend/moderation/outage/retry common shocks stay together. An unjustifiable or
uninformative partition, crossed shock, or unusable balanced unit makes both affected endpoint
families ineligible or inconclusive. A nonstructural zero
bootstrap variance is inconclusive. Source is not a sampling stratum. Every
internal frame has unweighted source caps only; each assigned internal population has unweighted and
`q*` share caps, and no full-frame `q*` exists. The binding source-shift functional is
the RMS across commonly scaled coordinates of each source group's exact `q*`-weighted median shift
from its complement, compared with the auxiliary-census bound and repeated under uniform weights.
Primary results remain specific to the observed source mixture, and exact common
source×scene-group comparisons are required diagnostics only where supported.
