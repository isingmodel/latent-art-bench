## Executive assessment

This is a strong bounded empirical paper. It now tests its most interesting historical observation prospectively: whether the same two generated-only maps receive opposing preferences from reference energy and corrected prediction of named scene means. On the new 12-scene FLUX/Cézanne panel, both contrasts instead favor the unchanged translation/scaling map. That is substantive new evidence against extending the historical ordering, not merely a better explanation of already observed data. It answers the principal future-study question in my Round 3 review, although under a changed finite-repeat evaluation design rather than an identical-estimand replication.

The resulting contribution is a useful empirical account of how actual painter clauses, simple global maps and different evaluation targets behave across these recorded comparisons. It is not the discovery that marginal and conditional evaluation differ, nor a general rule predicting their agreement. The new result appropriately narrows the argument. The four-painter context, original OAuth non-rejections, one actual generic-clause success, retrieval declines, both unresolved palette collections, failed planning proposal and failed platform replay remain visible.

No numerical or layout blocker was found for the stated bounded conclusions. One minor interpretation edit would distinguish nonrecurrence under the prospective evaluation from a demonstrated cohort effect. Substantive limits remain in measurement meaning, stochastic-service assumptions, breadth of transfer and public numerical portability. Documenting these limits does not resolve them.

## Concrete strengths

**S1 — A genuine prospective challenge to the central historical observation.** Section 7.3 freezes the old maps, representation, references, endpoints and analysis before new generated outcomes. Its contrary result alters what the article can establish. This adds more scientific information than another favorable sensitivity view on the original vectors would have supplied. The value is the challenged prediction and outcome, not the output count or significance alone.

**S2 — The targets and comparison units are unusually explicit.** Tables 8, 11 and 12 distinguish full-cloud energy, separate fold averages, corrected mean mismatch, occupancy and retrieval. The finite-R V-statistic target is identified, and the new uncertainty calculation deletes paired repetitions while preserving scene masses. This helps readers avoid interpreting different numerical quantities as interchangeable measures of style.

**S3 — The global-map argument contains controlled, interpretable comparisons.** Section 6.4 separates an old-origin displacement from a fixed-center scalar change. Appendix J.1 extends that result analytically along the same path without claiming an optimal scalar or fitting another grid. This is a useful local deduction, despite being elementary rather than a new general method.

**S4 — Important adverse findings remain scientifically active.** Sisley's different named/free ordering, substantial held-fold variation, original OAuth non-rejections, retrieval declines and both palette uncertainties are retained. The new same-direction E/Q result appears in the abstract and main table rather than being confined to an appendix. These observations constrain the account rather than serving only as caveats.

**S5 — The actual-clause and map comparisons answer distinct questions.** The 96-image OAuth C/G study tests a real alternative clause; the 240-image FLUX F/N study evaluates fixed feature maps. Keeping their controls, memberships and inferential contracts separate avoids turning the strong C/G p-value into evidence for a map mechanism. Failed original clause endpoints stay unavailable.

**S6 — Numerical evidence is concretely inspectable.** The fifth public package has a supported replay of all 27 qualification cells and the actual components, deletion estimates, variances and intervals; fresh local and anonymous checks agree exactly. All nine figures are readable; Tables 11 and 12 are now adjacent on page 18, making the changed ordering easy to compare.

## Genuine weaknesses and residual limitations

**W1 — Measurement validity still materially limits the reach of the evaluation lesson.** Sections 3, 5.5 and 9 use a selected, correlated 31-coordinate geometry. Texture contributes approximately 47–49% of reference trace, and one LBP coordinate dominates the controlled blur response. The common-square and feature-omission checks establish selected sensitivities, not capture equivalence or an independently validated stylistic construct. The five-work metadata audit finds no qualified capture pair, so it adds no measurement-validity evidence. This is not a demand for human ratings or a different learned representation; it explains why the present conclusions must remain about these image coordinates. A future capture study would require two documented photographic events for the same physical works, with ancestry/view/condition distinctions, rather than more transforms of one reproduction.

**W2 — The prospective result supplies a boundary, not a transferable explanation or selection rule.** One selected painter/service and twelve authored scenes now support a same-direction result. The paper still cannot predict when T1 or T2 will be preferred, whether another artist/service shares that behavior, or why the historical result changed. In addition to scene/time changes, Table 11 uses fold-fitted maps and four 18-image energies, while Table 12 uses full historical fits and one 120-image transformed cloud; E's finite-sample target changes. This does not invalidate the prospectively fixed comparison. It limits its replication and explanatory significance. The immediately feasible remedy is precise wording, not retrospective refitting or another representation grid; broader causal explanation would require a genuinely separate design.

**W3 — The new interval procedure remains an approximation with consequential service assumptions.** Ten repeats per scene improve the opportunity to estimate repeat variation, but independent stationary paired-repeat errors are not established by random order, complete delivery, software tests or an opaque service label. The 27 proxy laws are finite historical constructions with only 64 joint support points per law. The actual Q half-width is substantially wider than their baseline median criterion. That observation does not diagnose a tail mechanism, prove failed coverage or warrant discarding the result; it does limit how confidently proxy qualification can be presented as predictive calibration. Conditional-on-old-map inference also omits uncertainty in choosing and fitting those historical maps. The present report is honest, but the limitation remains substantive. No post-result interval substitution, optional sample extension or invented stationarity test is a remedy.

**W4 — The control and palette studies do not identify artist semantics separately from delivery and operating range.** The successful generic comparison is one phrase pair on OAuth, with 16 versus six medium-quality reports and no contemporaneous free arm. Those delivery differences belong to the assigned whole-service outcome; adjustment by them would not automatically recover a semantic effect. The two palette levels likewise leave gain, shift and saturation unresolved, even across two collections. These results usefully restrict interpretation but cannot explain the broader contraction mechanism. Removing this limitation would require a separately planned common F/G/N delivery comparison or a multi-level response design, not a claim that more repetitions of the existing extremes resolve it.

**W5 — Public reproducibility is now specifically limited by a failed platform execution, as well as excluded pixels.** Appendix K documents actual same-Mac numerical replay. The unchanged Ubuntu checker nevertheless fails before reaching observed E/Q, and its retained exception does not identify the differing field. It is unwarranted to assert that support hashes alone failed or that the discrepancy is harmless rounding; the record also does not establish that the experimental estimates are wrong. This is a practical access limitation for readers outside the verified runtime, beyond a merely hypothetical portability caveat. A separately versioned diagnostic comparator that identifies the first mismatch would be useful engineering work if pursued, while preserving the failed run, scientific source, tolerances and outputs. The absent image/response bytes additionally prevent public re-extraction and acquisition authentication.

W1–W4 are genuine scope or evidential limitations, largely requiring different future evidence to remove. W5 has a concrete software-diagnostic route. None is a justification for reopening a terminal study, regenerating results, changing a frozen criterion, or collecting more images to raise a score.

## Literature positioning

The closest primary sources support the manuscript's restrained novelty claim:

- **Su et al. (2025), Sections 3.2–3.5 and 9.1–9.2:** fixed-content artist substitutions, held-out prompts, named/free comparisons and real-artist prototypes precede this paper. The present distinction is evaluating generated-only moment maps against both distributional reference proximity and repeated-scene organization. Its novelty is not artist-name control or prompt holdout itself. [Primary version 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.18633v1).
- **Benny et al. (2021), Sections 3.1–3.2 and Theorem 2:** conditional evaluation already separates between-condition organization and within-condition matching; unconditional scores can conceal conditional failure. The current Q is not their conditional FID: it compares transformed free outputs with actual named scene means, without scene-matched real paintings. This distinction supports the paper's empirical application and limits any general-method claim. [Primary journal article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11263-020-01424-w).
- **Zhang et al. (2025), Sections 4.2–4.3:** increasing specificity of reference-grounded captions can reduce conditional diversity while improving reference-based measures. Contraction with improved proximity, by itself, is therefore established prior art. The present clause intervention and fixed-map prospective outcome add a more specific empirical comparison. [Primary version 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.19557v1).
- **Khayatkhoei and AbdAlmageed (2023), Section 3/Propositions 3.1–3.2:** k-nearest-neighbor precision/recall can behave asymmetrically for inward/outward support displacement in high dimensions. These results motivate caution but do not prove distortion of this paper's different reference-ball occupancy in 31 coordinates. Section 2 now states that distinction accurately. [Primary ICML paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/khayatkhoei23a/khayatkhoei23a.pdf).
- **Diedrichsen and Kriegeskorte (2017), crossnobis-estimator discussion:** independent-partition products remove positive squared-distance noise bias; whitening is an additional operation. The paper correctly attributes the product principle without claiming that its Euclidean Q implements the full whitened estimator. [Primary article](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005508).
- **Lakens (2022), precision/sample-size sections:** precision planning requires both a justified desired width and assumptions about variability. The existing single citation is sufficient. It does not certify this study's proxy laws, t9 calibration or achieved width. [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1525/collabra.33267).

I conducted targeted searches and read these primary text sections, not merely search snippets. This is not an exhaustive systematic literature review. Search surfaced an apparent ICLR 2026 version of Zhang, but its OpenReview endpoint returned a browser challenge; I did not use its unread content or recommend a mandatory bibliographic change on that basis. The fresh Project Euclid Efron–Stein page exposed no substantive text; my earlier retained reading covered web-extracted introductory variance formulas, not a local PDF or inspected source-page pixels. The current citation appropriately credits the classical jackknife; neither it nor this review supplies a theorem establishing this study's stratified t9 coverage. I found no essential missing nearest-source attribution requiring another related-work expansion.

The contribution level is a **strong empirical application with a useful prospective counterexample**, moderate in the skill's broad significance taxonomy. The new collection improves evidential substance; it does not turn the work into a landmark metric, general theory or validated explanation of artistic imitation.
