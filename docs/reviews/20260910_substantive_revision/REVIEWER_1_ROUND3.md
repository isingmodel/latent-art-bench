## Executive assessment

The paper now supports a coherent empirical contribution: painter naming can improve a specified digital-reference proximity score, while the generated variation and conditional scene organization do not follow uniquely from that improvement. The strongest explanatory evidence remains the observed disagreement between fitted predictors: adding a scalar improves estimated named-scene-mean prediction in all six primary cells but worsens reference energy in five. The common-mean comparison and later translation result make that disagreement concrete.

Round 3 adds a useful **prospective comparison against an actual generic painting clause on 24 new scene briefs**. Its separate Cézanne cohort is complete, has a strong prespecified conditional randomization result, and retains the failed predecessor rather than treating its absent endpoints as null results. This closes one real control limitation for one painter and one generic wording. It does **not** supply the unavailable prospective map/conditional-structure validation, a Monet generic comparison, or a general painter-naming effect across scene populations.

The numerical and reporting contracts are strong. I found no mathematical defect requiring withdrawal or correction of the reported results. The public addenda now resolve the concrete access gap identified in my round-2 review. Nevertheless, the reference-capture and feature-weighting limitations, and the limited precision/generalization of the post-result diagnostic rankings, remain scientifically material. I support publication as a scoped empirical article with minor presentation improvements; I do not classify all its scientific limitations as minor merely because they are disclosed accurately.

## Concrete strengths

1. **The new generic control answers an actual alternative explanation.** Section 5.7 compares two generated clauses, with otherwise unchanged fresh scene text and no palette extremes. It therefore contributes more than another sensitivity view of the old data. The lower reference energy is large in the fixed geometry and appears in all three specified processing views, although only the primary view carries inference.

2. **Failure handling preserves the estimand instead of filling it after seeing results.** Appendix A.3 retains all 288 original slots, 211 images and both unavailable endpoints. The separate successor retains the refused brief, regenerates both arms, fixes two repetitions and alpha .025, and permits no further replacement. Its decision predates feature inspection and the later predecessor stop. This is sound separation of cohorts; it does not itself prove the conditional service-null assumptions.

3. **The diagnostic comparisons have real discriminating content.** Sections 6–7 show that a map closer to the named scene means may be farther from paintings, and that an unchanged translation can outperform actual naming on reference energy. The common-mean comparison removes a specific ambiguity about transferred center offset. For later Monet, the scalar penalty is about .591 energy units while the old-center offset reduces it by about .161; old anchoring alone therefore cannot explain that penalty.

4. **Finite-repeat mathematics is handled carefully.** Q uses off-diagonal cross-repeat products, B* removes finite-repeat mean noise, negative estimates remain possible, and the appendix distinguishes fixed-map conditional assumptions from joint independence across overlapping fits. The omission of corrected Q for evaluation-centered T2c is necessary because that centering couples residual repetitions. There is no invented confidence interval based on four folds or dozens of correlated sensitivity views.

5. **Public computation is now concrete and versioned.** Appendix K names the new modules and the clause adapter separately from the historical package. The actual combined archive preserves unavailable and available cohorts exactly. Local and anonymous-download receipts report the same numerical/report hashes, 152 passing tests and eight explicit maintainer-only skips; none of those skipped private workflow tests is represented as public pixel authentication. This directly resolves round 2's access weakness.

## Remaining genuine weaknesses and their implications

### W1. The reference-distance target is still substantially shaped by measurement and capture

Sections 3, 5.5 and 9 define a legitimate fixed geometry, but correlated digital-texture coordinates account for about half the reference trace. The processing challenge is markedly nonselective: resampling produces a texture displacement much larger than JPEG 95, and blur changes spatial as well as texture features. Unknown reproduction workflows remain. Matching central squares changes visible content and cannot create independent-capture equivalence.

The new successor also has different delivered dimension and quality distributions by clause: 16 generic versus six Cézanne outputs report medium quality, with all 96 nonsquare despite identical requested geometry/quality. These fields need not explain the effect, but the experiment does not separate them from it. Thus the result is a delivered-service feature-distribution effect, not an isolated stylistic or content-preserving response. Scope wording is correct; the scientific meaning remains limited.

**Action:** Preserve this target definition. A stronger art-specific interpretation needs a separately specified measurement study, for example multiple independently captured reproductions of the same works and a justified content-sensitive representation. Human ratings are not required for the current numerical claim. Post-treatment filtering on delivered quality would not repair the current experiment and should not be added.

### W2. The central conditional-versus-marginal ranking remains post-result and precision-limited

The new clauses answer a proximity question, while Sections 6–7 still reuse original templates and post-result diagnostics. Three repeats enable unbiased corrections under stable independent errors; they do not establish the error law or show how often the ranking would recur under fresh generation. A negative corrected fold, large naive-to-corrected changes and heterogeneous held-scene residuals make this a material evidence limitation. Fixed-fold scene deletions answer conditional stability, not global remaining-scene or fresh-service uncertainty.

**Action:** Keep the reported comparison descriptive. If the next objective is a stronger ranking claim, freeze the current T1/T2 maps and a parsimonious pair of conditional/proximity targets before collecting an additional fixed new-scene panel with both free and named arms and sufficient repeats for a qualified error procedure. That would be a new study, not a repair by bootstrap or a reason to run another retained-data sweep. No generic effect interval should be attached to this sharp-null test merely to fill a reporting template.

### W3. The new control's scope and the paper's broader contribution remain limited

There is one successful fresh generic comparison: Cézanne, one generic wording, one service, one authored panel and the retained 32-reference target. The originally planned Monet and conditional-map endpoints are unavailable. The contrast also changes panel, time, repeat count and control relative to the earlier OAuth study; its larger magnitude cannot be credited specifically to generic wording. These are missing scientific comparisons, even though the failed attempts are reported impeccably.

The broader insight that conditional organization and marginal proximity can disagree is established. The present contribution is the concrete observed behavior of simple generated-only maps under painter-name interventions, not a new general evaluation principle or an identified explanation of generation internals.

**Action:** Keep the specific observed counterexamples central. If wider clause conclusions become the objective, a new prospective study with a small set of independently specified generic wordings and painters would discriminate control dependence. Do not interpret a second Cézanne success or more processing views as a substitute for those comparisons, and do not revive or pool the closed predecessor.

### W4. Numerical reproducibility is excellent, but full empirical reproducibility is still unavailable

The three releases allow meaningful computation from compact observations. They omit raw artwork, generated pixels and provider responses, so a reader cannot independently re-extract the feature vectors or authenticate the service/acquisition from the public package. The clause addendum's two successful fresh environments are on the same macOS arm64 host using locked cached packages; they are not an independent laboratory or a newly demonstrated Linux replay.

**Action:** Retain these explicit boundaries and public source/rights metadata. Additional platform testing can improve portability but will not resolve measurement or acquisition validity. A rights-compatible pixel subset or independent acquisition would be a separate artifact/data decision, not something a checksum or another numerical audit can supply.

## Literature assessment

The manuscript's restrained positioning is consistent with the primary sources checked in this round:

- [Su et al., Identifying Prompted Artist Names from Generated Images, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.18633v1), Sections 3.2 and 3.5, already uses fixed content with artist substitutions, named/free image comparisons, held-out prompts and real-artist CLIP prototypes. Therefore neither artist-name substitution nor generated/reference comparison alone is novel. This paper's repeated-scene distributional targets and predictor disagreement are a narrower, distinct empirical analysis.
- [Benny et al., Evaluation Metrics for Conditional Image Generation](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11263-020-01424-w), especially Sections 3–4 and the weighting discussion, already separates within-condition behavior from condition organization and exhibits differing marginal/conditional model rankings. The present paper applies that established concern to painter prompting; it does not establish the general distinction for the first time.
- [Zhang et al., The Intricate Dance of Prompt Complexity, Quality, Diversity, and Consistency in T2I Models, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.19557v1), Sections 1 and 4, reports reduced conditional diversity alongside reduced synthetic/reference shift as prompt complexity increases. Holding the scene description fixed and changing a painter clause is a meaningfully different intervention, but co-occurrence of proximity improvement and reduced diversity is not itself new. The manuscript explicitly cites the pinned preprint; a current venue citation is optional bibliographic housekeeping, not a novelty change.
- [Diedrichsen and Kriegeskorte, Representational models](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005508), the independent-partition distance and validation sections, supports the lineage of off-diagonal products for removing positive squared-noise bias. Its distinction between fixed-condition and population-condition validation also supports the paper's restricted interpretation. This paper's Q is unwhitened Euclidean, not a crossnobis estimator or a general model test.
- [Parmar et al., On Aliased Resizing and Surprising Subtleties in GAN Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.11222), and [Khayatkhoei and AbdAlmageed, Emergent Asymmetry of Precision and Recall](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/khayatkhoei23a.html), support caution about processing and neighborhood metrics. They do not establish that a specific observed difference here is caused by capture or the asymptotic high-dimensional phenomenon. The manuscript appropriately treats them as motivation rather than proof of that attribution.

I also checked the institutional metadata/abstract for Deliège et al.; current full-text retrieval encountered access/timeout problems, so I do not claim a fresh complete reading of that source. The paper's expert-rating target is correctly distinguished from a fixed measured image corpus. I found no material uncited novelty claim requiring result changes, and I did not re-authenticate every older artwork or bibliography entry.
