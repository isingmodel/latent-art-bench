## Executive assessment and fixed scores

The manuscript now supports a stronger **bounded empirical account**: actual painter clauses can improve proximity in the specified feature geometry; simple fitted transformations do not consistently reproduce the measured effects; and the historical disagreement between two evaluation targets fails to transfer to one genuinely new fixed scene panel. Its result constrains the earlier interpretation: on this panel the unchanged shift/scale map improves both targets. ## Specific strengths

1. **The new panel can contradict the historical interpretation.** The maps and two targets were fixed before the 240 new outputs; no evaluation centering or refitting restores the earlier sign pattern. This supplies a meaningful prospective constraint rather than recycling the original folds. The failed R4/R6/R8 proposal, the disclosed pre-data R10 redesign and the wider observed interval are all preserved separately.

2. **The estimands and dependence units are unusually explicit.** The paper distinguishes empirical V-energy from expected finite-R energy, scene-mean Q from latent noise-free observations, equal-scene retrieval from reference-content weighting, and assignment inference from model-based intervals. The paired jackknife keeps every scene weight fixed and deletes both members of a repetition. It does not use an invalid permutation of deterministic map labels.

3. **The actual-clause controls provide evidence beyond a metric illustration.** Same-scene artist-free contrasts across three services, later FLUX recurrence and the separate Cézanne/generic comparison establish specific measured service responses. Retaining the failed four-arm cohort without pooling or survivor substitution protects the estimands. The generic comparator is genuinely informative for the narrow comparison even though it does not settle all painter-specific effects.

4. **The diagnostic argument avoids several common errors.** Contraction's contribution to energy is given with the correct sign; coverage and retrieval are not interchangeable; finite-repeat corrections retain negative estimates; and the centered-map dependency prevents an unjustified Q correction. The elementary scalar-path result gives an explanation with no new fitted search.

5. **Public evidence is substantial and inspectable.** Exact inventories, source/input lineage, compact measurements, unavailable dispositions and separate arithmetic/replay checks allow considerably more scrutiny than code alone. The new prospective table matches the recorded direct audit at floating-point precision. All nine figures remain readable; the expanded Figure 3 and adjacent historical/prospective tables make the key comparison easier to assess.

## Genuine remaining weaknesses

**W1 — Measurement and capture remain the largest interpretation limit.** Equal IQR scaling does not make correlated color/spatial/texture coordinates equally meaningful; texture supplies roughly half the reference trace, and processing can move these coordinates strongly. The no-texture sensitivity even reverses one naming-energy direction. Common-square cropping changes reference content and cannot identify the original photographic contribution. The five-work metadata audit found no qualified capture pairs; it supplies no capture effect estimate. These limits matter even with the improved “painting reproductions” title: the computational distances are well-defined, but why the distributions differ and how much the results inform painting style remain unsettled. Human ratings are not necessary to establish the stated numerical contrasts; they also cannot be silently inferred from them.

**W2 — Prospective observations do not close actual-service uncertainty.** The new intervals use an approximate stratified jackknife/t construction with independent stationary paired-repeat errors. The 27 finite-support laws preserve useful historical covariance but are bounded, constructed proxies estimated from three historical repeats. They do not represent every plausible tail, between-block dependence or backend drift. The realized Q half-width of 2.833 versus the baseline planning median limit 1.0 is an observed limitation of precision prediction; it is not by itself proof of interval undercoverage, and a median proxy criterion was never a realized-width guarantee. Both intervals exclude zero under the frozen procedure, but nominal 95% cannot be described as empirically established service coverage. Merely choosing conservative degrees of freedom or passing simulations does not prove it.

**W3 — Scientific reach remains narrower than the amount of analysis might suggest.** The prospective map study is one historically selected painter/service and twelve authored briefs, with the original full-cohort maps, not a probability sample of scenes or model runs. The historic and prospective comparisons also differ in evaluation panel, time and fitted-map construction relative to fold averages. The reversal therefore identifies contingency without explaining its cause. The generic result concerns one clause and painter, and reported service quality differs by arm; the failed original cohort supplies no two-painter comparison. Existing evidence cannot support a service-invariant painter-name mechanism, a universal conditional/marginal conflict, or a general advantage over generic painting instructions.

**W4 — Reproduction is strong on the maintainer's platform but incomplete in two practical ways.** The public data cannot re-extract features from source pixels or authenticate acquisition/delivery. More immediately, the published strict Ubuntu attempt fails at the complete qualification comparison, and its coarse error message leaves the numerical leaf unknown. It is not justified to attribute the failure solely to support hashes or harmless floating-point drift. Since the checker stops there, its successful artificial tests give no Ubuntu credit for the actual E/Q table. These are realistic access/portability limits, even though the manuscript states them accurately.

**W5 — The narrative is accurate but still demanding.** A 39-page article combines exploration, original assignment tests, historical diagnostics, two kinds of prospective study, palette inference, measurement challenges and five numerical archives. The abstract carries nearly the whole chain of findings. Readers must distinguish several class-weight conventions and uses of the word “coverage.” Table 8 and the caption qualifications help substantially, but navigating the result/release hierarchy still requires sustained work. This is a minor presentation concern compared with W1–W4, not a reason to discard the ancillary evidence.

The original contribution is best described as a detailed, reproducible empirical study of **specified painter-clause effects and contingent evaluator rankings**, with an informative prospective failure of historical sign transfer. The scalar lemma and repeat corrections strengthen explanation and estimation but should receive no independent originality premium. The new result establishes neither a general reversal phenomenon nor why it happened.

## Prioritized actions and limits

1. **Editorial/access guidance using existing evidence:** place a compact five-row archive-to-result guide beside data availability, with the platform status of the newest addendum visible before its command block. State “same-Mac exact replay; Ubuntu qualification comparison failed; observed replay unreached.” This would help a reader choose the right archive and understand an actual failed command without changing any sealed package.
2. **Editorial compression:** shorten the abstract around the three central empirical statements: controlled clause effects; historical separation of the two targets; new-panel agreement of those targets. Keep the palette and measurement qualifications, but move some cohort/count and diagnostic detail to the already effective design table. Preserve the explicit negative findings and unavailable endpoints.
3. **Future numerical maintenance, separate from this review and frozen evidence:** a later additive diagnostic could report the first differing qualification leaf and distinguish tolerant floating fields from exact support/discrete fields. Preserve the failed hosted record and original strict contract. Until a supported successful attempt exists, do not claim Linux replay or infer its cause from the platform difference alone. This is a public-release diagnostic task, not a request for new images or new scientific estimands.
4. **Future scientific work only if broader claims are desired:** the priority is an independently characterized capture comparison and an acquisition design capable of assessing service/session dependence or prediction of repeat precision. Another retained-data feature sweep or more repeats in the same panel will not resolve those issues. New-scene/painter generality requires a separately chosen panel or sampling frame. These are limits on future claims, not instructions to reopen any terminal cohort, acquire images, add human ratings, or conduct another score-driven revision.

Two questions remain scientifically useful for future work: which acquisition or feature-family differences account for the measured domain gap, and which aspects of the new collection explain the changed map ranking and unexpectedly wide Q interval? The present records do not identify those answers. The manuscript is strongest when it retains them as open questions rather than presenting its considerable numerical verification as a substitute.
