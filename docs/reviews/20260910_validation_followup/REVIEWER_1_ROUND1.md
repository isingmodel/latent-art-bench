## Executive assessment

The revised paper provides strong evidence for a narrow but useful result: adding the selected painter names reduces the specified empirical feature discrepancy in the tested FLUX scene panel, and this direction repeats in a newly collected output set. The two palette collections still leave the additional painter-name response relative to the selected generic clause unresolved. The computational challenges now give direct evidence about what the 31-feature representation responds to; they also expose substantial processing response and overlap among the named feature families. These are substantive new results, rather than extra caveats attached to the original study.

The main scientific conclusions are supported at their stated finite-panel scope. I found no numerical, assignment, multiplicity or inference-gating defect in the independently checked new results. The manuscript generally avoids converting feature proximity into artistic fidelity, rejection into a mechanism, or unresolved palette interactions into equivalence. Remaining scientific limitations are material: the capture pipeline is incompletely separated from the reference target, the challenge set does not validate artist-style relevance, and the new collection covers selected exposed scenes and services rather than transfer or variability across collection dates. The immediate manuscript defect is premature present-tense public-release language while the release and external CI are still pending.

## Specific strengths

1. **The additional collection tests a previously observed result with genuinely new retained outputs.** Its allocation, ordering, four-endpoint family, missingness and duration rules were fixed before collection. All 264 assigned images returned in 64.3 minutes without retries or failures. This supplies evidence beyond replaying old vectors, while preserving the distinction between temporal repetition and investigator independence.
2. **The measurement challenge has informative controls and honest failure modes.** Exact PNG identity, calibrated chroma changes, two blur doses, JPEG compression, resampling and tile rearrangement are all reported. Stronger chroma and blur doses produce larger designated-family displacement for all 70 works. The full matrix reveals that spatial and color responses are not selective, and the manuscript identifies the LBP-coordinate concentration rather than presenting a large texture response as broad validation of twelve texture coordinates.
3. **The statistical units and dependencies are explicit and implemented correctly.** The palette unit is a complete eight-cell repeat block, not an image; shared generic controls enter the covariance; six fixed scenes do not become a random scene sample. The two new naming comparisons reuse 24 free outputs and are adjusted together with the palette endpoints. Frozen primary records are distinguished from nested diagnostic calculations.
4. **Distributional diagnostics resist an overly favorable reading of naming.** Matched-size comparisons, real-split coverage, cross-painter alignment, retrieval and representation sensitivity ask different questions. Their disagreements provide scientific information instead of being suppressed in favor of a single favorable scalar.
5. **Traceability is unusually detailed for a small empirical study.** Source, environment, identities, features, requested/delivered configurations, ledgers and terminal receipts are retained. My direct V-energy and single-pair-swap reconstruction agrees with the new naming implementation within 2e-14; independently reconstructed palette covariance, degrees of freedom, intervals and four-test Holm results agree within 2e-13. These checks materially strengthen confidence in the reported computations.

## Substantive weaknesses and residual limitations

1. **The new challenge validates sensitivity to chosen digital operations, not artist-style validity.** The selected transformations are partly aligned with the definitions of the measured coordinates. They were fixed before the new extraction but chosen using already-exposed reference works. Moreover, blur affects several families, tiles affect color statistics, and 83.9% of mean squared texture displacement under the first blur dose comes from one LBP coordinate. Thus a positive known-change-minus-processing comparison cannot establish selective composition, texture or style measurement. This limits the interpretation of both the original and repeated naming effects even though the current text mostly handles it correctly.
2. **Common-square cropping resolves only one component of reference/service comparability.** Reference capture, prior resizing, compression, color handling, borders and potentially source selection remain associated with the real/generated distinction. The square removes substantial image area, with reference medians about 75% for Monet and 78.8% for Cézanne, while already-square FLUX and NB2 images are unchanged. Preserved signs are useful evidence against one geometric explanation, not evidence that capture effects are absent. Establishing that requires additional capture-controlled data or paired source renditions.
3. **The temporal extension improves repeatability more than generalizability.** It retains the same 24 scene templates, two reference panels and feature scale. There is one new FLUX output per arm and scene, and only one fresh interleaved collection. It cannot estimate new within-prompt variability, a population of collection dates, transfer to unseen paintings/scenes, or independent provider behavior. The 24-hour gate and pacing enforce a design boundary; they do not empirically establish independent block errors or stationarity.
4. **The palette result remains specific to a narrow manipulation and control clause.** Only the specified vivid/muted contrast and one generic art-style clause are studied. The primary intervals still permit scientifically nontrivial positive or negative additions. The repeated negative generic-minus-free secondary response is useful, but does not identify painter-specific prompt conflict or show a general attenuation law. Reported OAuth quality and geometry also differ across arms and collections; these are part of the delivered service effect, not covariates that can safely be adjusted away after treatment.
5. **The contribution remains an empirical application, not a validated mechanism or broadly established principle.** Low-dimensional feature discrepancies, fidelity/diversity diagnostics and processing sensitivities have precedents. The distinct contribution is their controlled combination with naming, a generic comparison, preserved evidence and a temporal repeat. This is useful, but still centered on a limited set of painters, prompts and services.
6. **Public reproducibility remains incomplete and is currently overstated.** Availability text cannot substitute for accessible numerical artifacts. The planned package intentionally cannot reproduce pixels, collection or reference acquisition from numerical tables; that boundary is scientifically reasonable when stated, but actual third-party execution of the numerical route remains to be demonstrated. This is a release task, not a demand to redistribute restricted images.

## Qualitative methodological assessment

The primary weighting, development-only feature scaling, finite-sample V-energy definition, paired randomization algebra and complete-grid logic are coherent. I found no need to repair a frozen estimate, change an endpoint, exclude an output, or reinterpret a diagnostic as a new primary test. The two temporal FLUX directions are well supported under their randomization null; the palette intervals are approximate and conditional on the retained collection assumptions, not uncertainty over an artist's oeuvre or a population of dates.

The common-window result is a substantial retained-evidence sensitivity because it remeasures every relevant image and recomputes the full eight-contrast family. The ten-condition challenge is also new measurement evidence, but its per-work bootstrap ranges remain descriptive resampling summaries of exposed works. Neither procedure licenses perceptual equivalence or population coverage. The paper's definition of digital feature proximity is therefore essential to the meaning of its numerical claims, rather than a limitation statement that makes all measurement concerns disappear.

The practical distinction among a delivered service, its reported model identifier and an independently controlled backend is handled appropriately. Exact nonduplication, ledger completion and measured dispatch spacing help establish collection integrity; they cannot establish independent latent draws. Keeping the quality/geometry mismatch in the estimand is preferable to post-outcome filtering. No new inferential defect is identified by those descriptive delivery differences.

## Targeted primary-source context

I searched for nearby work on generated-painting stylistic evaluation, interpretable separation of real/generated paintings, and resizing/compression effects. I read the accessible primary text of Parmar, Zhang and Zhu's arXiv manuscript, sections on CLIP separation and interpretable/inversion probes in Asperti's current preprint, and Naeem et al.'s official proceedings abstract. This supplements the primary literature read in my preceding review; it is not an exhaustive novelty search.

- Parmar et al. demonstrate that resizing implementations and encoding choices can materially change generative-image evaluation. That makes the present paper's processing challenges relevant, but their results do not independently validate this paper's 31 coordinates or painter-style interpretation. [Primary manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2104.11222v1).
- Asperti studies separation of real and generated paintings using CLIP, interpretable statistical representations and inversion. This is a close conceptual neighbor for statistical separability; the present paper's experimentally changed naming clauses and generic comparison are a different intervention. Representation movement in either setting is not automatically a measure of artistic fidelity. [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.25609v1).
- Naeem et al. distinguish fidelity and diversity diagnostics and propose density/coverage measures. Their official abstract supports the importance of not reducing distribution evaluation to one discrepancy. This review does not claim that applying those ideas in the current handcrafted space makes their perceptual interpretation automatic. [Official proceedings page](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/naeem20a.html).

Access limitation: the current PMC page for Deliège et al. returned a browser challenge, and the Europe PMC full-text XML endpoint returned an error during this reassessment. I do not base a new novelty judgment on its search snippets. Parmar's accessible HTML is the earlier arXiv version, not a newly verified reading of every change in the later CVPR version. Asperti is a preprint, not peer-review certification of the present measurement choices.
