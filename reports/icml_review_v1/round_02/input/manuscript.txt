1548   These records establish the conﬁguration submitted to the gateway, not the identity of hidden served weights. Provider
1549   catalogue records are bound to the precollection freeze, but the absent echoed ﬁelds prevent response-level conﬁrmation of
1550   model and quality. The labels therefore denote requested conﬁgurations. The earlier interface’s acceptance of an invented
1551   model name motivated this explicit-route collection; it does not prove that valid names selected identical models. The
1552   terminated predecessor and technical probes are excluded from the 1,008-image analysis.

1631   K.5. Request-time sensitivity
1632   The recorded request starts exactly follow the randomized allocation, but randomization occurred within contiguous scene
1633   blocks of 72 requests. Global collection time is therefore confounded with scene identity. Repeat labels do not encode
1634   chronological order: repeat 1 started later in 271 pairs and earlier in 233, with a median within-cell separation of 188.8
1635   seconds.
1636
1637   A retrospective diagnostic differences the 31-dimensional measurements within each model–scene–arm cell and ﬁts a
1638   common linear drift vector against the signed start-time difference. Each conﬁguration has 84 such pairs. Slopes are trained
1639   on 13 scenes and evaluated on the omitted scene. Relative to predicting zero difference, held-out squared-error gains are
1640   −0.86%, −3.38%, −2.28%, −2.62%, −0.72% and −1.19% in table order; every complete scene-deletion reﬁt also has
1641   negative gain. This particular common linear drift model adds no predictive value. It neither establishes independent
1642   or stationary responses nor excludes nonlinear, arm-speciﬁc or shared-state effects. No primary score is detrended, and
1643   these are descriptive sensitivities, not additional signiﬁcance tests. The new result can be replayed with python -m
1644   latent_art_bench.painter_request_timing_v1 check.
