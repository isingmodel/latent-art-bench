1760   M. Retrospective held-scene prompted-name identiﬁcation
1761
1762   M.1. Question, chronology and information budgets
1763   The criterion is the recorded painter name in each generation prompt, among Monet, Sisley, Pissarro and Cézanne. It is not
1764   physical authorship or an expert judgment of resemblance. Baseline recognition and its confusions were already known
1765   when this diagnostic was chosen. Its plan, implementation, constructed tests, embedding archives and original analysis
1766   bindings were then frozen before computing any translated outcomes. Thus the ﬁt excludes held-scene images, but the
1767   choice of question did not originate from an untouched test set. We call this a retrospective out-of-fold diagnostic, not a
1768   conﬁrmatory validation experiment.
1769
1770   The reference baseline uses historical examples only. Common translation additionally uses a balanced pool of generated
1771   named-arm examples, with no correspondence between individual training images and painter labels in its arithmetic.
1772   Selecting that pool and verifying its balance requires experimental metadata. The supervised generated-prototype rule
1773   additionally uses those individual painter assignments. These are different information budgets; the supervised result
1774   describes available generated-domain label structure, not a like-for-like zero-shot improvement or alignment with real-art
1775   features.
1776

2865   R.2. Coverage-matched comparison and operational criterion
2866
2867   Within a conﬁguration’s 112 named queries, let K be the number accepted by the reference gate. The comparator keeps
2868   exactly the K greatest top-two prototype margins, breaking ties by frozen observation ID. It uses no true prompt name in
2869   selection. This is a transductive descriptive comparator: its cutoff uses the query distribution, whereas the reference gate’s
2870   thresholds use historical artworks. Predictions remain the original top-1 assignments. For controls, apply the resulting
2871   numeric margin cutoff inclusively, retaining boundary ties. If K = 0, neither named queries nor controls are accepted; if
2872   K = 112, the minimum named margin is still the cutoff for controls. No prompt label determines either selection rule.
2873   The pre-outcome operational criterion requires at least 50% named-image coverage in every conﬁguration, at least one
2874   accepted query from every prompted painter in each conﬁguration, and positive equal-conﬁguration mean reductions in
2875   accepted error relative to both unrestricted top-1 and the coverage-matched comparator. These joint descriptive conditions
2876   prevent a nearly empty accepted set from being credited as useful. Undeﬁned accepted error is retained as a failure,
2877   without dropping its conﬁguration from an otherwise favorable mean. All individual adverse results remain visible. The
2878   50% threshold is this study’s declared operating requirement, not a universal criterion for useful selective classiﬁcation.
2879
2880   For every encoder/view/target/conﬁguration, retain the individual scores, candidate sets, predictions, decisions, painter-
2881   speciﬁc coverage, accepted and abstained error, and correct/incorrect counts. Delete each scene once to describe sensi-
2882   tivity, without changing historical calibration or interpreting the ranges as sampling intervals. Control-arm results report
2883   name-assignment rates separately for artist-free and generic-painting images. These rates concern assignments without a
