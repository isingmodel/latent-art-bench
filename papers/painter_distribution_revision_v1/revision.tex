\section{Post-result methodological revision}
\subsection{Declared scope and measurement alternatives}
After the controlled results and skeptical review, a separate namespace fixed
the diagnostic methods, source, tests, input hashes and seed before running the
full revision. These choices were informed by exposed results: this is not a
retrospective preregistration. Every new result is descriptive, with no additional
confirmatory tests, significance claims or replacement of the original eight-test
family. The calculations read retained vectors and metadata, without opening
pixels, extracting new features or making provider requests.

Four fixed feature views are applied to all three processing pipelines: original
31 coordinates; 28 coordinates omitting three derived summaries (deltaE slope,
wavelet slope and curvature); equal coordinate-count family weighting, dividing
color/spatial/texture coordinates by $\sqrt{11}$, $\sqrt{8}$ and $\sqrt{12}$;
and 19 color/spatial coordinates without texture. Family weighting does not
equalize observed variance or validate style relevance. All 168 cell summaries
and 72 named/free contrasts are retained. Energy magnitudes across differently
scaled views are not directly comparable.

For generated vector $z_{cbr}$, class $c$, brief $b$ and repeat $r$, let $w_c$
be its class mass, $u_{b|c}$ its conditional brief mass and $v_{r|cb}$ its
conditional repeat mass. With corresponding means, total trace decomposes as
\begin{align}
 T_G={}&\sum_{cbr}w_cu_{b|c}v_{r|cb}\|z_{cbr}-\mu_{cb}\|^2\nonumber\\
 &+\sum_{cb}w_cu_{b|c}\|\mu_{cb}-\mu_c\|^2
 +\sum_cw_c\|\mu_c-\mu\|^2.\label{eq:decomposition}
\end{align}
These are within-brief, between-brief within-class, and between-class components.
Between-brief-means trace combines the last two. Reference trace has only
within/between-class components; original brief labels are not invented. These
are exact weighted finite-set identities, not unbiased variance-component
estimates from three repeats. Coordinate/family trace contributions, development
correlations and four development-painter-omission scalers accompany the results.
Alternative scales use development data only.

Primary original-31 influence deletes each reference work, each exact set of
recorded holding-collection IDs, or each content class. Work/source deletions keep
the original class masses where support permits; class deletions renormalize the
remaining target. An exact collection-membership set is a source proxy, not a
documented photographic workflow. Deletion ranges are not confidence intervals.

Under common one-third class masses, the painter-prompt/reference double contrast is
\begin{equation}
 I=\mathcal E(X_A,G_A)+\mathcal E(X_B,G_B)
   -\mathcal E(X_A,G_B)-\mathcal E(X_B,G_A).\label{eq:specificity}
\end{equation}
Reference-only and generated-only within terms cancel. Negative $I$ indicates
relative own-painter alignment in these features, not human recognition. We also
calculate $I$ for the separately collected artist-free groups and their pairwise
energy, checking exact canonical payload identity for all 72 matched slots per
route. This collection-label placebo is not a calibrated null or an equivalence
threshold.

Coverage uses $k=1,3,5$ and 100 fixed-seed draws, half/full reference sample sizes
and uniform/class-count-matched generated sampling. Per-anchor radii,
same/cross-class hits and all memberships are retained. A separate control splits
originals into disjoint anchor/query sets with $\lfloor N_{pc}/2\rfloor$ per class
and matches generated query counts to those anchors. This defines a different
neighborhood target from full-panel coverage; its 18/15-work groups are not
independent replications across draws.

A square metadata rule and within-stratum feature/descriptor associations assess
nuisance plausibility. No causal adjustment is claimed without common support.
Directed cross-route detection uses the inherited fixed linear/RBF kernels and
ridge, six disjoint original-work/whole-brief folds, and all 24 shared-condition
route/painter cases. We report pooled fixed-threshold balanced accuracy and
per-fold AUC, never a pooled AUC across differently fitted margins. Verified
capture-workflow holdout is explicitly unavailable. All eight original endpoints
also receive descriptive initial-only, component-boundary, over-120-second
group-span, and combined boundary/retry exclusions. OAuth spans include all three
randomized conditions. These exclusions do not repair interference. The fixed
seed is 2026090719, with stable named substreams and no seed search.

\subsection{Aggregate contraction does not imply within-brief contraction}

results support aggregate concentration, but not uniformly reduced stochastic
variation at a fixed prompt. Three repetitions give limited information about

\subsection{Metric and reference choices bound robustness}
All 72 named/free total-trace ratios remain below one across the fixed metric and
pipeline grid. This does not mean every named collection is less dispersed than
its original reference. Without texture, NB2 Monet's named/reference ratio exceeds
one in the primary and JPEG pipelines. Its primary named/free energy change is
only $-.0175$, reversing to $+.4159$ at 256 pixels. In contrast, both FLUX painter
comparisons and NB2 C\'ezanne retain negative energy directions in all 12 variants
per comparison. OAuth C\'ezanne reverses to $+.0499$ in the primary 28-coordinate
view; OAuth Monet changes direction in some 256-pixel views. No preferred view
is chosen from these outcomes, and no new p-values are attached.

Primary reference texture accounts for approximately 49.0\% of Monet and 47.1\%
of C\'ezanne trace, despite representing 12 of 31 coordinates. Derived-coordinate
redundancy and correlations mean coordinate count is not a measure of independent
stylistic evidence. Removing texture also removes substantive visual information,
so its exception demonstrates measurement dependence, not invalidity of texture.

The four paid-route primary energy directions remain negative through all
specified work, collection-group and content-class deletions. OAuth C\'ezanne
crosses zero in single-work/source omissions; deleting a content class can reverse
either OAuth painter comparison. All four development-painter omissions retain
the paid directions, while omitting C\'ezanne changes its OAuth estimate to
$+.0042$. These are sensitivities of a finite comparison, not population
uncertainty intervals or independent source validation.

\subsection{Relative painter alignment and the collection-label placebo}
Primary named interactions $I$ are $-1.755$ for FLUX, $-.855$ for NB2 and
$-1.802$ for OAuth. Artist-free interactions are $-.048$, $+.101$ and $-.044$.
Named-minus-free interactions are $-1.707$, $-.956$ and $-1.758$, respectively.
All canonical artist-free payload pairs match; separately collected outputs
nevertheless have positive V-energy (.102, .079 and .077). Stochastic sampling
and service timing can produce nonzero empirical gaps under identical payloads.

The double contrast removes within-distribution terms from an uncalibrated
nearest-reference comparison. It supports relative alignment for these two
references, without establishing human recognition, controlling fine content,
or furnishing a zero-discrepancy threshold. OAuth alignment remains conditional
on its treatment-associated rendering.

\subsection{Coverage and detection depend on the comparison design}
At $k=3$ and full reference query counts, class-count-matched named coverage
medians for Monet/C\'ezanne are .711/.703 for FLUX, .447/.313 for NB2 and
.447/.438 for OAuth. OAuth Monet illustrates a change of direction: all-available
coverage falls from .553 free to .474 named, whereas class-matched medians rise
from .421 to .447. Coverage grows with neighborhood size. Across $k=3$ anchor
links, 50.0\% for Monet and 63.5\% for C\'ezanne cross broad content classes.
C\'ezanne's three water originals force every $k=3$ water-anchor neighborhood to
include at least one other class. A hit is not class-conditional content fidelity.

With disjoint reduced anchors, $k=3$ real-query coverage medians are .944 for
Monet and .933 for C\'ezanne; named generated medians range .500--.833 and
.400--.800. At $k=5$, both real medians and C\'ezanne FLUX named reach 1.0.
This coarse neighborhood saturation does not establish equivalence. Repeated
draws are finite-panel diagnostics, not independent reference replication.

Figure~\ref{fig:revision-coverage} presents this diagnostic.

Every paid output is square and every reference nonsquare, giving a square rule
perfect original/generated balanced accuracy on all eight paid cells. This does
not prove the feature classifier uses shape; it shows why absolute detectability
cannot establish a stylistic difference. All 70 capture workflows are unresolved,
and every delivery host is a Wikimedia thumbnail service. Holding institutions
are not capture identities. Paid prompt arms share square geometry, strengthening
their relative comparison without isolating a pure style mechanism or removing
prompt-induced content differences. Constant/missing descriptors make paid-route
nuisance correlations unavailable, not zero.

Across the 12 directed route/painter pairs, artist-free cross-route balanced
accuracy ranges .618--.924 for linear and .620--.887 for RBF detection. Named
ranges are .410--.874 and .447--.704. Naming lowers transfer accuracy in every
direction for both kernels; three named linear and four named RBF cases fall
below .5. Monet FLUX-to-OAuth linear transfer remains strong (.874), so this is
uneven fixed-threshold transfer, not universal detector failure. Ranking summaries
can move differently: Monet FLUX-to-OAuth linear mean within-fold AUC increases
from .9534 free to .9676 named, despite its balanced-accuracy decline. Calibration
or threshold shifts can therefore contribute; the result is not a uniform loss
of discriminability. High within-route separation does not imply a calibrated,
route-invariant detector. Below-chance transfer
is a domain-shift result, not perceptual authenticity. Sixty of 144 case-fold
instances lack an original content class; per-fold AUCs describe varying supported mixtures,
whereas pooled balanced accuracy restores full finite target masses.

\subsection{Execution sensitivity}
Research attempts span 11.527 hours. Median initial randomized-group spans are
25.153 seconds for FLUX, 11.812 for NB2 and 49.950 for OAuth; maxima are
50.781, 915.340 and 953.871 seconds. Two groups cross collector boundaries; the
same groups exceed 120 seconds. All eight endpoint directions remain negative
under every specified timing/retry deletion. The FLUX C\'ezanne initial-only
estimate is $-.8938$, versus $-.8956$ with its selected retry. These checks
preserve finite estimates but cannot test or repair unrecorded backend carryover.

\section{Discussion and next validation target}
The supported claim is that painter naming changes the tested empirical feature
distributions, with improved primary proximity on both paid routes and lower
aggregate spread relative to their artist-free controls. The revision explains
why this should not be translated into universal style collapse: within-brief
variation can increase, omitting texture changes one paid proximity direction,
a named/reference spread ratio can exceed one, and fixed-threshold detector
transfer loses balanced accuracy under naming. Agreement among metrics derived from the same coordinates
does not supply independent validation.

FLUX has stable negative energy directions throughout this bounded grid, but a
service ranking across painters, scenes or perceptual criteria is outside scope.
The two-painter interaction supports relative alignment in these coordinates,
while human style recognition and original-relative perceived variation remain
unmeasured. Prompt content, reference selection, sampling and service rendering
remain part of the observed comparison; a hidden model mechanism is not identified.

The next expansion axis is reference and construct validation. The retained
metadata frame contains 112 candidate identities, all nonsquare. Forty-two are
outside the panel, but include an explicitly ineligible painting and unadjudicated
candidates; they are not 42 ready additional references. Only one collection has
same-content cross-painter support, one land work per painter, and this is not
documented photography. A new source/content frame and declared precision target
must precede an acquisition count. More generated outputs cannot fill these gaps.

The human follow-up design specifies 72 pairs of six-image named/free sets from
retained outputs, preserving shared brief/repeat dependencies, with separate
resemblance and nuisance judgments. Designs with 24 or 36 raters are feasibility
options, not a power result. This can assess generated-only perceived variation;
three C\'ezanne water originals cannot supply multiple disjoint six-image original
boards balanced two per class. More raters would not repair missing reference
replication. Stimulus-manifest execution, recruitment, consent, institutional
review and judgments remain unperformed. Learned representations may provide
another view later, but agreement alone cannot validate perception.
