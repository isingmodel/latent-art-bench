# One prospective fixed-map redesign after offline precision failure

2026-09-10. This decision precedes the v2 qualification and every new generated
outcome. It is part of the existing paper-and-analysis revision, not a new claim
that v1 succeeded. Maintainer-run LLM agents advise, implement and review it;
they are not separate human investigators or institutional reviewers.

## Why reconsider the allocation

The v1 proposal is permanently stopped. Its frozen 96/144/192-output assessment
passed every coverage and baseline energy-width criterion, but failed baseline
conditional-residual precision at each allocation. At 192 outputs the two
failures were 1.018978 and 1.040286 against the median half-width tolerance 1.0.
There was no missing trial, numerical defect, generated image or empirical
map-transfer observation. The full failed result remains at
`studies/painter_map_validation_v1/pmvqv1-20260910/`, committed at `9e3fd1a`;
its source is `eb70ab8c30019b4b283366c170f8bdaf1405a023`.

The unanswered scientific question remains useful: on new authored scenes, does
adding the historical FLUX/Cezanne scalar improve corrected prediction of named
scene means while worsening painting-reference energy? The retrospective pattern
does not establish its transfer. Additional repetitions can reduce sampling
uncertainty for this question. The redesign therefore changes the planned
repetition count, while retaining the comparison and precision requirements.
It does not change endpoints or choose a more favorable painter after new outcomes.

This is an openly simulation-informed pre-data redesign. It is not outcome-blind
with respect to the historical measurements or v1 simulations. It is also not an
independent-investigator replication. A new name alone would not justify it.

## Relationship to the stopping commitment

The v1 contract says to stop "this inferential proposal" and prohibits changing
its method, seeds, scenarios, thresholds or allocations in response to failure.
It immediately provides for a "future separate proposal" retaining that failure
and its reasons. Protocol 2.1 section 15 permits a new protocol version before
protected outcomes are viewed. These clauses permit a disclosed new prospective
version; neither requires the scientific question itself to change.

The coordinator and reviewing LLM initially interpreted the prohibition more
broadly. On reading the two clauses together, they corrected that interpretation:
v1 stays closed, while a separately committed redesign before new image outcomes
can be assessed. No old document, result, source hash or stopping decision is
rewritten. This interpretation is disclosed rather than presented as an
allocation already permitted by v1.

Sample-size guidance distinguishes resource limits, precision planning and
power calculations; accuracy-based planning requires justification of the width
target and variance assumptions ([Lakens, 2022](https://doi.org/10.1525/collabra.33267)).
Guidance on preregistration changes emphasizes disclosing the change, its timing
and consequences; changes independent of the data used to test the hypothesis
need not introduce outcome-dependent selection
([Lakens, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1525/collabra.117094)). These sources support the
general methodological distinction. They do not attest this service's sampling
assumptions or approve this particular design.

## Fixed bounded assessment

- The sole candidate is **R=10 paired repetitions on each of 12 scenes**, or
  **240 outputs**. It is fixed now; no v2 precision result has been inspected.
- Keep the historical full-FLUX Cezanne maps, 32 reference works, 221-work scaler,
  primary512/all31 representation, class masses and unchanged free/named prompt
  scaffold. Exact new briefs and assignment must be frozen before collection.
- Reuse the same 27 finite-support historical proxy laws, support construction,
  interval procedure, seeds and 10,000 trials per law. The trial seed includes
  R=10, an allocation absent from the original grid. No alternative seed or
  method is tried if this assessment fails.
- Retain the v1 coverage lower-bound floor .94 and median baseline half-width
  limits E<=.25 and Q<=1.0. Retain the conservative Monte Carlo tail .05/81 for
  the new 27-cell assessment; do not make a combined 95% Monte Carlo statement
  about every bound across both versions. Report all stress widths and failures.
- The Q target remains a fixed-scene conditional-mean mismatch contrast. The
  energy target is now the **expected finite-R=10 empirical V-energy contrast**;
  increasing R changes that sampling target. Do not say all estimands are
  unchanged or pool it with the v1 hypothetical targets.
- Prepare and review the exact v2 qualification protocol/source before its
  create-once formal assessment. A pass concerns only these proxies. Actual
  service coverage, independence and stationarity remain assumptions.

This is the **single additional pre-data allocation assessment** for this map
question in the current revision. If it fails, do not try R=12, new thresholds,
different proxies or another method/version to obtain admissibility. If it
passes, collection still requires a separately fixed and tested transport,
assignment, measurement and analysis contract. An incomplete collection, contrary
ordering or wide observed interval is retained; it does not trigger a replacement
cohort, pooling, aesthetic retries or more repetitions.
