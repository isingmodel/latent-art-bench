## Cost, dispatch and failures

Baseline **$68.50735** = $67.5219185 historical conservative accounting +
$0.8348915 from the terminated 31-image attempt + $0.0289 from four low-quality
pilots + $0.12164 from four medium-quality probes. The historical $5 uncertainty
reserve remains included. Never query remaining credit. At the observed medium
costs and fixed NB2/FLUX quotes, the 1,008-image forecast is approximately **$112
cumulative**, including the historical reserve, before small prompt-token
variations. This leaves room for bounded retries within the requested ceiling.
This forecast is subordinate to the strict cumulative **less than $120** guard.
Reserve $5 per active paid attempt and settle only known charges. An unknown or
unexpectedly large charge pauses dispatch for diagnosis, retaining the reservation.

At most three requests overlap, starts at least five seconds apart. The request
order and exact payloads are fixed before collection. All starts and terminal
outcomes are durably appended; raw responses and pixels stay under the ignored
workspace. No visual/feature selection and no model fallback. SIGINT or an
operator pause file stops dispatch and drains active requests before returning.
A crash with an unmatched start requires explicit evidence reconciliation.

Retry at most twice for completed 429/500/502/503/504 or network errors, identical
payload, 20/60-second delays and at most 24 retries in total. Do not automatically
retry refusals or other client rejections. Three consecutive technical failures,
an invalid request contract, a model mismatch, or a financial uncertainty pauses
for diagnosis; a repair must preserve the declared scientific target and document
its effect. A pause is not a terminal census. The create-once terminal receipt
closes generation permanently. No feature measurement occurs before closure.

The fixed inferential family contains the six slopes and 15 paired model
distortion differences. Absolute distortion intervals are descriptive nominal
95% summaries, not additional claims covered by that 21-comparison family.

Missing named measurements make their model/scene repeat-corrected endpoint
unavailable. Report all missing cells; paired inference uses at least 12 complete
paired scenes and actual degrees of freedom n-1. Report the common complete-scene
panel across all models as well. Available-scene estimates may be affected by
nonrandom missingness and do not restore a missing complete-grid estimand.
