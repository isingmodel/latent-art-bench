## Scientific implementation findings

1. **Assignment and estimand agree.** Common constructs 48 complete two-position
   blocks from all 24 unchanged scenes and two repetitions, with independently
   randomized C/G positions and randomized block order. There are 96 fresh
   requests, disjoint IDs and exact predecessor C/G payload counterparts. The
   refused brief remains present. The analysis matches by scene/repetition,
   uses all 31 coordinates, gives each generated observation its class mass
   divided by 16 and each of the 32 references weight 1/32.
2. **The statistic includes distribution spread.** The primary sign is
   Cezanne-minus-generic reference V-energy. Both generated within-cloud terms
   remain in the paired-contribution identity. I inspected that inherited
   identity and the randomization primitive. Tests independently use direct
   double sums, enumerate every assignment in a small weighted design and
   replay the specified 48-pair Monte Carlo stream. The primary uses 99,999
   draws, conservative absolute ties, plus-one correction, seed 2026091052 and
   the fixed .025 threshold. No second endpoint or Holm adjustment is added.
3. **Unavailable observations cannot rescue the comparison.** Every assigned
   slot/pipeline must have terminal accounting. Any missing primary C/G value
   withholds the entire endpoint without complete-case renormalization. A
   missing secondary-pipeline value does not discard a complete primary.
   Literal true duration and identity flags and integer planned count 96 are
   required for inference. A complete estimate remains descriptive after a
   failed global gate; its p-value is not calculated. Missing-arm and failed
   gate cases are covered by tests that forbid randomization execution.
4. **Secondary scope is bounded.** The three pipelines report complete-arm
   weighted energy and observed traces, generated/reference trace ratios and
   C/G trace ratios. Incomplete inputs or zero denominators yield unavailable
   ratios. There are no maps, corrected variance components, retrieval,
   intervals, secondary tests or replacement endpoints. Delivery summaries
   count each output once and do not filter on reported quality.
5. **The inferential limits are explicit.** The decision is availability
   triggered, not wholly outcome blind. The .025 plus .025 joint error statement
   is conditional on valid applicable tests, including validity under the prior
   availability decision. Arithmetic alpha allocation does not establish those
   assumptions. Sharp joint availability/feature invariance and no interference
   are stronger than equal energy. The report preserves these distinctions,
   keeps the original Cezanne endpoint unavailable and leaves original Monet's
   Holm-two procedure unchanged.
