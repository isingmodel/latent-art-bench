## Scientific question

On a fixed new scene panel, do the **unchanged historical FLUX/Cezanne maps**
retain their opposing orderings under painting-reference energy and corrected
conditional-mean prediction error? Translation and translation/scaling predict
the same named arm from the same free outputs. The expected pattern is higher
reference energy but lower corrected prediction error after adding scale. Either
reversal, agreement of the two orderings, uncertainty, or missingness is a valid
outcome; none triggers a replacement or an outcome-dependent extension.

This is the central map-ranking question identified by all three round-3
reviewers. It differs from another painter-name significance test. Cezanne and
FLUX are chosen for a focused extension of the existing fixed maps, an observed
ranking disagreement, and affordable transport on the same named route.
Selection limits generality. The four-painter exploration remains in the paper;
this single painter/service experiment cannot stand for the other painters.

## Minimal design to qualify before collection

- Twelve newly authored briefs: four water, four built and four land scenes,
  distinct from both previous authored panels. Freeze exact wording before new
  outcomes. These are a fixed panel, not a probability sample of scenes.
- Artist-free and Cezanne arms with unchanged earlier prompt scaffolding. The
  same fixed service alias/provider and transport parameters are required.
- Compare four, six and eight repeats per scene/arm offline: **96, 144 and 192
  outputs**. Select the smallest allocation passing the fixed qualification
  criteria. Do not choose the allocation from new image outcomes.
- Only the existing primary 512-pixel, all-31-feature representation, fixed
  development scaler, 32 Cezanne reference works and reference-content masses
  `(water, built, land) = (3, 11, 18)/32`. Each new scene has one quarter of its
  class's mass.
- Apply the original full-FLUX Cezanne map pair used in the later transfer
  analysis. No new means, scales, feature weights or reference objectives are fit
  to evaluation data. Each source and numerical input must be bound explicitly.
- Retain full weighted empirical V-energy difference `E(T2 F) - E(T1 F)` and
  untruncated cross-repeat residual difference `Q(T2) - Q(T1)`. The first
  sampling target is its expected finite-repeat empirical value; the second is
  the difference in weighted squared conditional scene-mean mismatch.
  Their units and targets differ. No map-label permutation is valid.
- Qualify one stratified paired-repeat jackknife procedure using fixed finite
  support proxy laws. Coverage must be checked across all declared stress
  cases; width tolerances apply to declared baseline cases. A passing simulation
  establishes behavior under those laws, not actual service coverage,
  independence, stationarity, or guaranteed precision.

The detailed qualification contract fixes all proxy construction, seeds,
criteria and failure rules before its formal execution. If no candidate passes,
this inferential proposal does not proceed to collection. Such a failure must
not be relabeled a successful validation or silently replaced with a different
analysis.
