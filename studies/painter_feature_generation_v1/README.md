# Painter Feature Generation v1

This is the active reboot study for the question:

> For one exact generative model and one R0a-frozen 24-template common-content prompt frame, (a) do
> registered painter-name requests meet availability and content-adherence requirements and (b),
> among technically analyzable near-copy-excluded returns, does the generated distribution reproduce
> the content-standardized feature distribution in that painter's R0a-randomized sealed-confirmation
> population of eligible digital painting surrogates?

There is one canonical plan: [`PROTOCOL.md`](PROTOCOL.md), protocol ID
`painter-feature-generation-v1/1.7`. The current compact readiness evidence uses schema
`painter-feature-generation-v1-data-readiness/1.8`.

The protocol directly compares generated and real distributions, tests specificity against every
fixed comparison painter, checks coverage and contraction, and keeps prompt effects, copying,
availability, and learned evaluators separate. It uses one finite `outdoor_place_landscape` domain
with four broad scene groups; the former six narrow submotifs are nonbinding diagnostics. R0a must
acquire, adjudicate, and analyze every work in a minimum 1,440-work internal finite frame: 360 per
painter, assigned by one frozen painter-level permutation to 72 development, 108 method-
qualification, and at least 180 sealed-confirmation works (288, 432, and at least 720 overall).
Optional external replication adds a complete 96-work census per painter, or 384 works, for at least
1,824 acquired and analyzed real works. A separate auxiliary same-work independent-capture census
contains every eligible work in its frozen auxiliary frame, must contain at least 32 physical works—
at least eight per painter, each with at least two independent captures—and is outside all internal
and external totals.

Generation is separate. Before any active content label is read, R0a hashes all 12 byte-exact
candidate complete frames, including every 24-template artist-free and named-placeholder string,
punctuation, language, negative prompt, insertion point, painter-name substitution table, scene label,
five content values, and render contract. No later stage, including G0, may rewrite them. After painter-level population
assignment, R0a selects one frame using only three nonredundant scene-group proportions and exactly
five predeclared binary visible-property means. Every named condition and shared artist-free control
must receive the same `R` in every template, so the four-painter request count is `120R`. The former
`R=16`/1,920-request scenario is retired because it cannot clear the boundary-safe availability gate.
Even the impossible best case requires `R>=25` and at least 3,000 requests while treating every
repetition as its own auditable independent unit; the actual Bonferroni allocation, independence-
unit audit, and whole-decision simulation require a larger, still-unfrozen `R`. A fixed deterministic
local map uses independent template-specific IID uniform seed lists with replacement and retains
chance duplicates. An opaque/remote endpoint instead uses `C` equal-size common-shock units, each
with `L` complete balanced template×condition waves, and `R=C*L`. For every complete real
development, qualification, confirmation, and registered-external population, R0a freezes the
jointly feasible full-population entropy projection `q*` to the selected common target before
feature access. `q*` is primary, the joint convex-hull, weight-cap, and effective-sample-size gates
are binding, and it is never refit; the uniform complete population is a mandatory sensitivity.
One common weighted-median/IQR transform is fit to the equal-painter mixture of the four complete
`q*`-weighted development populations and reused for every real, control, and generated vector. A
separately frozen binding realized-content entropy projection uses blinded output labels, applies
the same support gates, and cannot rescue a failed primary result. The contrasts do not define exact
joint real-work cells. R1a freezes the actual `R` without changing any real census size.

The four-painter design is currently **NO-GO**. A federated query found 3,190 Wikidata painting-item
candidates, not verified works. A separate traceable live-item audit found 43 all-content metadata
candidates—Yale 16, Paris Musées 9, Getty 10, and Minneapolis Institute of Art 8; Pissarro 11,
Monet 13, Cézanne 12, and Sisley 7. That artifact preserves the observed live item identifiers and
rights/geometry checks but is not a reproducible as-of-date source snapshot. None has been admitted
to the outdoor-place corpus. R0a must verify at least 360 eligible internal candidates per painter
and at least 24 in every broad scene group, along with every identity, rights, geometry, content,
source-robustness, and joint `q*`-feasibility gate. Optional external registration requires another
96 unopened-source candidates per painter and at least eight per broad scene group, or 456 combined
per painter.
Every internal frame and internal development/qualification/confirmation population must retain at
least four holding/capture groups per painter. The frame caps each group's unweighted share at 30%;
each assigned population caps both unweighted and `q*` shares at 30%. Multi-source frame scenes use
an unweighted 70% cap; assigned-population scenes cap unweighted and scene-conditional `q*` shares at
70%. External populations require at least two unopened groups and 70% overall unweighted/`q*`
ceilings. No full-frame `q*` exists. The primary result remains specific to the source mixture in the
frozen frame. The binding source-shift functional is the RMS across the one commonly scaled family
of the exact `q*`-weighted median shift between a source group and its complement; it is compared
with the auxiliary-census disturbance bound and repeated under uniform weights. Exact common
source×scene-group analyses are mandatory diagnostics only where both painters have support; they
are not primary sampling cells and do not license imputation.

Two independent eligibility coders view only frozen, masked derivatives whose long side is at most
512 pixels. This is honest real-pixel exposure, logged and firewalled from feature, method, and
generation analysts. Before adjudication, three-way visual-eligibility agreement must be at least
0.90 per painter and each coder's ambiguous share at most 0.10. On the union-eligible denominator,
broad-scene and each five-property three-state contrast must reach 0.85; the same gate is repeated in
every assigned internal and registered-external population, and each coder's season/illumination/
depth indeterminate share must be at most 0.20 in every required scope. A failure is R0a NO-GO;
adjudication cannot erase it. In G1b, every sealed-confirmation and technically analyzable generated
image is double-coded and the raw condition-specific 0.85/0.20 receipts are sealed before a third
blinded adjudicator creates consensus; failure makes the affected endpoint inconclusive. Analysis-
resolution qualification/final files remain inaccessible,
and the sealed final reference is not opened until G1b. R0a freezes one eligible finite population per
painter and one random permutation that assigns exposure roles: ranks 1–72 are development,
73–180 are qualification, and 181 onward are sealed confirmation. Those roles are disjoint, but
they are not later probability subsamples: every work in every released population is measured.
R0b releases the complete 108-work-per-painter qualification census without adding or redefining
eligible works. Primary energy uses a `q*`-weighted real-census summation inside the generated–real
term, a real–real finite component, and an equal-template generated U-statistic: only the two real-
side components are exact; generator expectations remain estimated. Generated quantiles weight each analyzable
output `1/(24 m_t)`. Generator-only
resampling keeps each real census and its weights fixed. Under a local deterministic map it resamples
whole seed-condition vectors within template; under an opaque/remote endpoint it resamples only whole
balanced common-shock units carrying all waves and template×condition outcomes. The binding realized-
content entropy projection is recomputed inside each replicate. The continuous max statistic excludes rates:
availability, adherence, and copy use separately alpha-allocated Bonferroni weighted-Hoeffding
bounds with conservative ratio bounds. A complete rate endpoint counts `A` lower, `A` upper, `J`
lower, and `K` upper as four directional events in `M_rate`. The same G0 partition governs both rate
and continuous inference, and the rate radius uses `sum_c W_c^2`. Independent local seed draws may define units;
remote request IDs/timestamps do not, so plausible provider episodes, batches, backend/moderation
states, outages, retry cascades, and other common shocks stay together. A crossed shock, unusable
balanced unit, or partition not aligned with fixed-template resampling makes both affected endpoint
families ineligible or inconclusive; R1a stresses clustered pixel/feature failures. Any
nonstructural zero replicate variance is inconclusive.
There is no active real-work IPW, SRSWOR estimator, or real-work bootstrap. Source is retained for
binding robustness tests but is not a sampling stratum.

The next authorized stage is the rate-limited R0a item-level census, complete real-frame and
auxiliary-census acquisition, firewalled content coding, candidate prompt-frame selection,
population assignment, and `q*` freeze, followed by release of only the complete 288-work
development population plus auxiliary census. Active admissions, active-study image downloads,
registered generation requests, generated outputs, and results are all zero, so R0a remains
**NO-GO**. After later freezes, G1a must execute and seal every request, attempt, failure, output,
and hash while the final reference remains unopened. G1b alone opens the complete sealed reference
and runs the frozen confirmation.

The older [`painter_features_v1`](../painter_features_v1/) study is a preserved real-image
measurement precursor. Its hash-bound protocol and four-file Collection Freeze 3 are evidence, not
the current research plan, and must not be overwritten or moved.
