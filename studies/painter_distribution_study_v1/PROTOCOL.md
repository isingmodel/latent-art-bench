# Painter Distribution Study v1: staged protocol

Protocol ID: `painter-distribution-study-v1/1.0`. Issued 2026-09-06 before new
generation or acquisition for this study. This is a new namespace; no historical
terminal study is reopened or repaired.

## Authority and scope

The user requested implementation of the research proposal and an English paper
prototype, supplied an OpenRouter key locally, and instructed the maintainer to
use its recommendations after the clarification questions. The adopted choices
are collection across multiple days, a computational paper with human evaluation
prepared for follow-up, and prospective adaptation of reference-panel targets
when the documented feasibility audit warrants it.

The main expansion is two additional generation families, Nano Banana 2 and
FLUX.2 Max, with one existing OAuth service comparator. New generation focuses
on Monet and Cezanne. The primary representation remains the existing 31
interpretable features and development-only scaler. The four original painters
remain in existing-data diagnostics. Learned features are supplementary and
deferred until the primary computational study is complete.

Maximum generation allocation: 1,008 research attempts and 18 technical attempts,
one requested image per attempt, including at most 588 OpenRouter attempts.
All paid activity in this study has a hard $75 operating ceiling; the remaining
$25 is not available without a subsequent user instruction. No automatic
fallback, best-of-N selection or image replacement is permitted. A failed attempt
consumes the request allocation even when billing is zero.

## Scientific questions

1. How different are original and generated feature distributions under a common
   declared content mixture?
2. Is a difference accompanied by reduced spread or coverage?
3. Does naming the painter improve specificity relative to artist-free generation
   and a different painter's reference?
4. How sensitive are these patterns to prompt content and digital reproduction?

These are separate questions. Detectability does not establish poor aesthetic
quality, intrinsic diversity collapse, or absence of recognizable style.

## Stage gates

### A. Existing numeric diagnostics: authorized now

Read the 649 measured original records, the complete derived 1,920-image prompt
grid and their original provenance. Run the fixed analysis in `DIAGNOSTICS.md`.
Reuse existing content metadata and disclose its lack of independent visual
validation. No image acquisition or feature extraction is required at this stage.
Commit the exact code/config/method inputs before preparing the analysis freeze.
Publish a new report and verify numeric and file-hash reproducibility.

### B. Reference and endpoint feasibility: authorized before generation

Audit existing exposure identities and public museum/open-collection metadata.
Record source URLs, retrieval times, raw-response hashes, attribution, rights,
physical-work identifiers and deduplication decisions. Metadata discovery may
use at most 1,000 GET requests and 250 MiB of retained response bodies under this
namespace. Authenticated OpenRouter reads may inspect model/provider capabilities,
pricing and account/key credit limits without exposing the key or private account
identifiers. No generation POST is authorized by a metadata-discovery command.

The initial reference target is 48 fresh works per painter in 12 supported content
strata, plus 12 independently captured same-work pairs per painter. These are
feasibility targets. The user authorizes prospective adaptation based on source
availability, content support and identity/capture evidence, before new outcomes
are measured. Preserve the audit and explain every change in a separate reference
contract. Previously exposed works must remain labeled exposed.

Image acquisition and measurement start only after that contract and a selected
identity manifest are committed and frozen. No more than 208 new artwork files
may be acquired without a prospective scope amendment. Mirrored files, crops and
alternate encodings are not independent works or captures. A website or museum
name is not an identified photographic workflow. The proposal's capture claim
must be reduced if genuine independent events cannot be established.

### C. Technical pilot: requires a committed pilot contract

Specify six technical requests per route, their exact prompts and payloads,
provider pinning where supported, complete request cost bounds, geometry checks,
response limits and storage accounting. The pilot is excluded from research
endpoints. Select operational settings without extracting pilot fidelity features
or selecting favorable-looking outputs. Bind transport code, endpoint snapshots,
configuration and authorization in a new freeze before the first POST.

### D. Research generation and measurement: requires a committed research contract

Freeze the selected reference panel, content rubric, prompt strings, endpoint
contract, schedule, analysis and uncertainty qualification before new evaluation
features are extracted. The proposed grid is 24 briefs x three repetitions x two
painters x named/artist-free conditions for each of three routes, with a generic
named condition on OAuth only. Any feasibility-based revision must precede new
research generation and retain the main questions and cost ceilings.

Use at least eight collection windows across multiple days and assign repetitions
of a brief to distinct windows. Record actual times; no claim of independent
backend sessions follows from scheduling. Interleave models and conditions.
Never convert elapsed runtime or a missing result into permission to resend.
Persist an attempt event before transport and retain all terminal outcomes.
An uncertain in-flight attempt remains uncertain until reconciled; no blind retry.
Closed runs are terminal; follow-ups use new IDs and disjoint paths.

The 512-short-side primary normalization preserves aspect ratio and follows the
existing color/profile contract. Symmetric 256-short-side and common-JPEG
sensitivities require fixed processing contracts and development-only scaling.
Do not normalize away palette or texture to manufacture overlap. Record render
deviations and keep OAuth separate wherever matched rendering is required.

### E. Analysis and paper

Report energy discrepancy, spread, secondary coverage, common PCA views,
group-disjoint separability and painter-name/control contrasts. Match declared
content weights and show collected-mixture results alongside them. Simulate the
actual reference/brief/window dependence before claiming calibrated inference.
Sample counts establish affordability, not power. If qualification fails, report
effect sizes and sensitivities without unsupported confirmatory claims. No
significance-based sample extension or endpoint selection is permitted.

Write an English, venue-neutral paper prototype with anonymous author placeholders,
complete methods, figures, citations, limitations and reproducibility instructions.
Human evaluation remains a prepared follow-up and is not reported as executed.
Kim is neither contacted nor listed as author without a later user instruction.
All review in this task is maintainer/LLM self-review, not institutional independence.
