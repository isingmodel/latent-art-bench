# Method decision ledger

Version: `painter-feature-method-decisions/2.0`

Status: literature-grounded prospective decisions aligned with Painter Feature Generation v1
Protocol 2.0. This ledger explains the plan; it does not independently authorize execution.

## Decision rule

“Retain” means retain for prospective qualification, not already validated. A revision trigger
requires a new protocol before protected pixels, features, or generated outcomes are used.

| ID | Decision | Evidence/rationale | Revision trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| MD-01 | Ask whether painter-name generation reproduces real painter feature **distributions**, not whether a classifier predicts painter labels | Classification can exploit content, source, borders, chronology, and pretraining; a centroid can hide collapse | A different explicit scientific question |
| MD-02 | Name the construct `broad-scene-weighted digital-surrogate feature reproduction` | Broad scene is standardized, but phase, iconography, season, light, depth, and accessible-source mixture remain in the target | A validated design that isolates or probability-samples those factors |
| MD-03 | Use the physical painting as the real unit; nest source rows, captures, assets, and files under it | Digitization studies and duplicate failure modes show files are repeated measurements, not works | An estimand explicitly about digital files |
| MD-04 | Preserve Pilots 0–3 and Painter Features v1 as historical evidence | Negative/terminal outcomes constrain the reboot; many records are path/hash bound | Never within this protocol |
| MD-05 | Close a named authority/discovery/media source union and exhaust every route | Count-stopping and source substitution make the finite target outcome-dependent | A new pre-request source registry |
| MD-06 | Remove incomplete Wikidata `P186` from broad discovery; verify oil and canvas from authority records | Missing federated material statements are not negative evidence | Demonstrated complete material coverage in the discovery layer |
| MD-07 | Keep actual unequal painter counts and every eligible work in the closed frame | Equal counts have no inferential necessity for complete finite populations and can invite weak provenance or discard lawful works | A probability-sampling estimand with known inclusion probabilities |

| MD-36 | Limit a positive result to one exact model/checkpoint/render/prompt/seed/request frame | Model services, prompts, and accessible art corpora are selective and mutable | A prospectively expanded model/prompt population |

## Candidate disposition vocabulary

- `qualified_primary`: every frozen coordinate and M0 gate passed;
- `diagnostic_only`: informative for shortcuts or evaluator sensitivity but nonprimary;
- `source_sensitive`: confirmation changes under a supported leave-workflow analysis;
- `content_or_domain_limited`: result is restricted to an explicit observed domain;
- `failed`: a required gate failed;
- `inconclusive`: the endpoint could not support a directional decision; and
- `not_executed`: its prerequisite was unavailable or closed.

No failed/inconclusive family is dropped from the all-three-family decision.

## Execution boundary

Protocol 2.0 authorizes stages, not an unrestricted workflow. Before each stage, its exact inputs,
outputs, software, denominators, stopping rules, and clean-output state must be hash-frozen and
independently reviewed. Metadata-only R0 cannot download images or admit works. R1 cannot calculate
painter features. R2 coders cannot see source/painter metadata. M0 cannot access confirmation arrays.
G1 cannot open confirmation features. C0 is the one-time frozen confirmation analysis.

Until those freezes exist, external image acquisition, active feature extraction, generation
transport, image generation, and confirmation remain closed.
