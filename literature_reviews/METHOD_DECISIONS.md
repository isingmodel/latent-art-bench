Version: painter-feature-method-decisions/1.15

## Decision rule

Each decision records the evidence-supported action, its rationale, and the observation that
could force revision. “Retain” means retain as a candidate for prospective qualification, not
declare valid.

## Candidate disposition vocabulary

Every coordinate receives one of the following after qualification:

- qualified_core: passes all gates required for a painter-associated coordinate;
- qualified_domain_limited: passes only for a declared source, phase, content, or medium domain;
- reproduction_associated: repeatable across the tested reproduction domain but not
  painter-specific under transfer;
- digital_derivative: stable only within derivatives of one capture;
- diagnostic_only: useful for shortcut, semantic, or evaluator-family sensitivity;
- replication_only: retained to reproduce a paper under its native assumptions;
- failed: does not meet its prospective gate; or
- not_executed: required inputs or artifacts were unavailable.

No failed candidate is silently removed from the report.

## Stop conditions before empirical execution

The literature review alone does not authorize acquisition or measurement. A frozen execution
protocol must still specify:

1. target painter set and historically defensible comparison painters;
2. eligible works and attribution policy;
3. four broad scene groups; all bytes and hashes for the 12 candidate complete frames, including
   both prompt renderings and render behavior, frozen before active labels; the selected equal-weight
   24-template generated target; the primary `q*`-content-standardized sealed-confirmation target;
   uniform-real sensitivity; source-mixture definition; and binding source-robustness diagnostics;
4. the separate at-least-32-work auxiliary reproduction census and independent capture-ancestry audit;
5. feature-card formulas, exact available artifacts, and adaptation labels;
6. perturbation SESOIs and terminal decision thresholds;
7. precision/power simulations, hard broad-scene support alarms, the exact real-census/generated-U
   estimator, generated-quantile weighting, realized-content entropy projection, one common pooled-
   development transform, and the local-seed/remote-balanced-unit generator-only resampling contract;
8. the 1,440-work complete internal frame, painter-level exposure-role permutation, full measurement
   of 72 development, 108 qualification, and at least 180 confirmation works per painter, fixed
   population `q*` vectors with joint feasibility receipts, the full visual-screening and union-
   eligible denominators, pre-adjudication agreement/ambiguity/indeterminate receipts, binding
   population-specific source-share and source-shift tests, plus a complete
   96-work-per-painter census only when external replication is registered;
9. equal named/control repetition counts, local template-specific deterministic seed lists or remote
   `C`-unit/`L`-wave balanced execution, the `120R` and `R=C*L` formulas, the analytic boundary-safe
   repetition floor, and whole-decision simulation that freezes the actual `R` before G0;
10. an experiment-wide continuous max-statistic construction separated from the Bonferroni
    weighted-Hoeffding rate bounds, with all four directional events per full rate endpoint and a
    shared pre-execution independence-unit map for rate and continuous endpoints, remote common-shock
    rationale, fixed-template alignment, clustered pixel/feature-failure coverage simulation, and
    prospective rule for nonstructural zero variance;
11. G1b double-coding, raw reliability-receipt, third-adjudicator, and failed-endpoint rules;
12. frozen denominators, missingness scenarios, and terminal transport rules; and
13. rights, storage, and redistributability.

Until that document is reviewed and committed, external-holdout access, feature extraction,
generation transport, and image generation remain closed.
